Project Overview
----------------
E-Flora is a B2B-B2C online marketplace designed specifically for the plant community, bridging the gap between plant nurseries (suppliers) and customers. It operates as a verified platform ensuring authenticity, quality, and smooth transactions.

Key Features & Capabilities
---------------------------
- Role-Based Access: Dedicated portals and workflows for Customers, Suppliers, and Admins.
- Supplier Verification System: Mandatory document verification (GSTIN, PAN, license) by Admin before a supplier can sell.
- Product Moderation Workflow: Admins must approve all plant and product listings before they go live on the platform.
- Real-Time Inventory Management: Accurately tracks stock to ensure transaction safety and prevent overselling.
- Comprehensive Order Lifecycle: Tracks everything from cart checkout and payment to order confirmation and delivery.
- Automated Invoicing: Generates robust, GST-compliant invoices (calculating CGST/SGST/IGST) and automatically emails them.
- Integrated Payments: Secure, seamless checkout experience.
- Ratings & Reviews: Allows customers to leave feedback on plants and nurseries.

Platform Workflows
------------------
1. Supplier Onboarding Workflow
   - Register as a supplier on the platform.
   - Complete the business profile and upload required verification documents (GSTIN, PAN, License).
   - Admin reviews the documents and either approves or rejects the application.
   - Once approved, the supplier gains access to their dedicated dashboard.
   - Supplier lists their products (each product then requires admin approval before surfacing to customers).

2. Customer Order Workflow
   - Customer browses the approved products and adds them to their cart.
   - Customer proceeds to checkout, selects their delivery address, and reviews the order.
   - Customer completes the payment securely.
   - Order confirmation is displayed, and an automated, GST-compliant invoice is generated and emailed.
   - Customer can track the order status from packaging to dispatch.
   - Final step: Delivery confirmation.

3. Admin Workflows
   - Vendor Management: Verify and approve/reject newly registered suppliers and their documents.
   - Catalog Moderation: Review and approve/reject all product listings added by active suppliers.
   - Order Oversight: Monitor and manage all customer orders globally.
   - Analytics & Reporting: View business analytics, performance markers, and comprehensive reports.
   - Access Control: Manage user accounts and platform permissions.

Layout & Colors
---------------
The application utilizes a nature-inspired, premium design system tailored for a plant marketplace:
- Primary Color Theme: Various shades of Green (from light mint #f0fdf4 to deep forest green #052e16). The core brand color is a crisp, vibrant green (#22c55e), perfectly reflecting the botanical theme.
- Accent Color Theme: Shades of Indigo/Blue (core accent #667eea) are used to provide an elegant contrast for buttons, text highlights, and interactive elements.
- Animations: The interface feels alive with integrated micro-interactions such as fade-ins, gentle pulsing, floating elements, and smooth shimmer effects to deliver a modern, premium user experience.

Recommendations for Production Readiness
----------------------------------------
To elevate the product and make it fully comprehensive for an enterprise-level launch, consider adding the following:

1. Advanced Search & Filtering: Ability to filter plants by care difficulty, lighting needs, pet-friendliness, and indoor/outdoor suitability.
2. Logistics Integration: Direct API integration with shipping providers (like Delhivery or BlueDart) for automated AWB generation and live tracking links.
3. Subscription Models: Allow customers to subscribe to routine deliveries of essentials like fertilizers, soil, or seasonal flowers.
4. Loyalty & Reward Program: Offer reward points for purchases or writing reviews to increase customer retention.
5. AI Plant Assistant: A simple chatbot or feature to help users pick the right plants based on their room size or diagnose basic plant diseases.
6. Multi-language Support (Localization): Support for regional languages to bring in a wider B2B and B2C audience.
7. Legal & Compliance Requirements: Visible Terms of Service, Return/Refund Policies, Privacy Policy, and Cookie Consent banners.
8. Customer Support/Helpdesk: Live chat or a ticketing system (e.g., WhatsApp Business integration) for quick dispute resolution and buyer support.
